# App Store Release Notes: Version 2026.10.0

Paste everything below the line into App Store Connect's "What's New" field. It's plain text (the field doesn't render markdown).

Made for iPhone Duo, with a new way to compare solving methods and a more reliable solver.

📱 BUILT FOR IPHONE DUO
Unfold your Duo and SpeedCube AR spreads out:
• Scramble and solve side by side: your scrambled cube stays on the left while the solution plays step by step on the right.
• Tutorials and the Algorithm Library side by side: follow a lesson on one half and look up algorithms on the other.
• Folded, everything fits the cover screen, clear of the status bar.
The same layouts work on iPad and when you turn your iPhone sideways.

⚖️ NEW: COMPARE METHODS (Pro)
Solve one scramble with every method at once (Layer-by-Layer, CFOP, Roux, ZZ, Petrus and Heise) and see move counts and stage breakdowns side by side, with the computer's optimal solution for reference. Tap any method to watch its solution step by step.

🧩 MORE RELIABLE SOLVING
• Roux now solves every scramble, and much faster.
• Heise no longer occasionally fails, and solves in about a second.
• Stage colours in the playback timeline are corrected.
